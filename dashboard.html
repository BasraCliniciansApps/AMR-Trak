Chart key
  • Solid bar: 30 or more isolates tested. The result is reliable.
  • Faded grey bar, name ending with *: fewer than 30 isolates tested. Interpret with caution.
  • ‡Intrinsic resistance (after the name): resistance is expected for this organism (CLSI M100 Appendix B), not acquired.
  • †Selective test (after the antibiotic name): tested on fewer than 90% of the isolates, so the percentage may be biased.
  • iTap a bar: shows n (number of isolates tested) and the 95% CI. The bar height is always the selected percentage.
